# Menus and shortcuts

The menu key defaults to **G**: press G while holding the tool to open the menu. The right mouse button is reserved for selecting points and construction. The other default shortcuts are listed below. After rebinding, follow the game's controls and item tooltip.

## **Using the menus**

* **Open radial menu**: change the binding under "Controls → GTLCore Matter Manipulator". With the tool in either hand, press the menu key to choose a mode or setting.
* **Radial menu**: click a sector to open or select it. Click the center or press Esc to go back.
* **Buttons below the menu**: open "Materials", "Network link", "Plans and orders", and "Task controls".

While selecting points or working, press G (or your menu key) to open the menu. If you rebind the menu key to right-click, release right-click first.

## **Selecting points and starting work**

* **Right-click a block surface**: select the adjacent block position outside that face.
* **Sneak + tap and release right-click**: select the targeted block itself. You can also aim at air to fill in a point; check the preview and coordinates.
* **Sneak + hold right-click for 1.5 seconds**: start or resume a task. A ring beside the crosshair shows the startup progress. Keep holding right-click to work; release it to pause.

## **Selection shortcuts**

* **Ctrl+C**: switch to Copy mode and mark the source selection.
* **Ctrl+X**: switch to Move mode and mark the source selection.
* **Ctrl+V**: start marking the paste selection and choose a new destination.
* **Ctrl+Z**: clear the source and destination coordinates.

These shortcuts only change the mode and selection settings. Hold right-click to start actual construction. **Ctrl+Z does not undo construction**; use "Task controls" to undo a task.

## **Sampling blocks**

* **Middle mouse button**: sample the targeted block state for all four material categories: Corners, Edges, Surface, and Interior.
* **Sneak + middle mouse button**: add the targeted block state to the replacement whitelist.
